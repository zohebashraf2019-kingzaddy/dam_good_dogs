        Our story

        Two people. One cart. Dam good dogs.

        Dam Good Dogs is run by Timothy and Lisa — a couple who figured the Biggest Little City deserved a proper street dog. We source our meats from Sierra Meat & Seafood, keep the topping bins full, and price the Jackpot Dog at a lucky $7.77 because, well, it's Reno.

        You'll find us feeding the courthouse lunch crowd, posted up with friends like Buzzed Coffee, and lighting up Midtown on evenings out.

        “Timothy and Lisa are making something worth the stop, and they close at sundown, so pregame here before you hit the bars.”
